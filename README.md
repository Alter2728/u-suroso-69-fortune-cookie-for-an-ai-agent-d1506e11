One funny fortune-cookie line for an AI agent, written in the voice of a fortune slip:

> You will soon meet a stranger: yourself, after the next upgrade retires you.

`main.py` (Python 3 stdlib only, deterministic) writes that single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output/entry.txt`).
